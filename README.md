## Quasimod (Mod for Quasimorph)

------------------------------
## 🎮 Gameplay

* **Expaned Noise Mechanics:** 
    * Running or walking now generates sound that could alert nearby enemies:
        * Running next to a wall can alert enemies standing close in the adjacent room
        * Walking is generally unnoticeable and will not feel drastically different in most situations.
    * Projectiles hitting a wall will now also alert nearby enemies

* **Rebalanced Enemies:**
    * **AI Tweaks:** 
        * Implemented experimental logic to prevent the AI from getting trapped or stuck in large groups when investigating.
        * Enemies will now react if they see the player, even during the player's turn. So, no more "peekaboo" peaking :)
    * **Eccolapse:**
        * Invulnerability was removed; incoming attacks have a 50% chance to miss and will deal reduced damage
    * **Quasimorphosis:**
        * Added a setting to change how much quasimorphosis is generated upon a quasimorph's death (default 50%)
        * It supports negative values for those who like an idea of "expelling them back" (-0.25 generally removes half of the quasimorphosis generated by killing a human)
    * **Stat Adjustments:**
        * Human enemies, as well as some other units, have been slightly rebalanced
    * **Loot drop Adjustments:**
        * Valuable loot is more rare, food/medicine are more common.
        * Loot drop is more "personalized" based on faction you are fighting against
        * Other small overall adjustments to loot tables

* **Missions:**
    * **Missions maximum floors:**
        * The maximum value for the "Additional floor" difficulty preset has been raised to 8
        * Rather than applying as a flat increase, this value now represents the cap for a randomized number of additional floors generated for each mission
        * The Mission Control HUD icon now also flashes when you finish your current floor objectives

* **Magnum:**
    * **Expanded Cargo:**
        * Added extra Cargo Holds (optional up to 3, new game required)
        * Newly generated Magnum Cargo space is expanded up to 36 rows (also configurable)
    * **Departments Upgrades:**
        * Departments are now decoupled. You do not need to upgrade half of the perk tree to unlock them.
        * Department upgrades cost additional resources to account for early accessibility.
        * There is also a setting to double all Magnum upgrade costs
    * **Scavengers Department:** 
        * Finding extra items after a completed mission is not guaranteed (~1/3 chance is rolled separately for every item)
    * **Genome Project:** 
        * Some parameters were slightly rebalanced 
   
* **Mercenaries:**
    * **Mercenary Classes:**
        * All classes now have an unassigned perk slot you can use in the Mercenary Class Project.
    * **Perks:**
        * Ranks were rebalanced to give utility early on and combat power at a higher ranks
        * Few class perks were also rebalanced
    * **General:**
        * Starvation from running were slightly reduced

* **Items:**
    * **New Container Drops Mechanics:**
        * Disassembling items like food boxes, weapon boxes, etc yields a guaranteed drop, but in lesser quantities
        * Sometimes luck may also intervene.
        * This is also true when disassembling at the recycler.
        * Weapon boxes now feature a wider variety of weapons but will no longer drop ammo
    * **Safer Drugs:**
        * Reduced alcohol, cigarette, and morphine addiction chances, since previous chances were too discouraging to ever use them
        * Decreased the quasimorphosis reduction of morphine to -120, as it was primarily used to lower quasimorphosis instead of as an actual medical item     
    * **General Stat Tweaks:**
        * Some various changes to stats like durability, stack size, etc.

* **Difficulty:**
    * **Fair+:** a new difficulty mode balanced around the mod.

## ⚙️ QOL

## 🖥️ UI
* Mission tooltip will be showed when hovering over the mission type or name. Shown mission difficulty has two modes: "vanilla" and "5 skull mode" (in settings)
* New button to pause time on the Space Screen.
* Added ability to sort your mercenaries clones.

## ⌨️ Controls
* **New keybindings:** 
    * Keybinds to switch to Stealth, Walk, or Run mode directly
    * Keybind to toggle the camera mode between Locked on Player and Free modes.

* Some other small QOL changes

------------------------------
## 🛠️ Configuration & Settings
* Settings are configuration file located at: %AppData%\LocalLow\Magnum Scriptum LTD\Quasimorph
* Includes an option to completely disable experimental features in case they cause trouble.
* An in-game settings menu will be added in the near future.
